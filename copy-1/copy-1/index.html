Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how , thewrenworks.com, uses the personal information we collect about you when you use this website/subscribe to the mailing list.

What information do we collect about you?
thewrenworks.com collects personal information when you sign up for The Wren Works Agency | Substack and is governed by the Substack’s  Privacy Policy.

Website usage information is collected using cookies. If you contact us, we may keep a record of that correspondence. If you send us personal information which identifies you via email, we may keep your email and email address.

How do we use the information we collect?

Website
Cookies are used on this site to  track the numbers of visitors to our site but do not collect any personal information or store IP addresses locally on our site.
Mailing lists
We will use the information collected to send you marketing emails which consist of: information about our products, blogs, podcasts, video and social media we share, and any offers or events we may be running.
You have the right at any to stop us contacting you for marketing purposes. If you no longer wish to be contacted for marketing purposes, click unsubscribe at the bottom of any marketing email we send or contact us via our site.

Cookies
Cookies are text files placed on your computer to collect standard internet log information and visitor behaviour. For further information visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.
We do not share your data
We do not share, outsource, or offshore your information to anyone outside our company and only use it to contact you. We respect your privacy and will not sell or pass your information to anyone unless required by law.

Where do we store your data?

On our website
Any information you submit via our website will be stored on our site and protected behind our hosting provider’s firewall. As with any such host’s website there is no absolute guarantee of security. If you feel this not sufficient then please do not provide any personal information or let us know and we will dispose of the information, securely of course.

How long do we keep your data?

Marketing Emails
We may occasionally remove data from our mailing list, if there has been a significant period of inactivity (ie no email opened for 1-2 years). If we do this then we will email you to let you know we are considering doing it, and you have the right to continue receiving mailings, or to allow us to go ahead.

Changes to our privacy policy
We regularly review our privacy policy and any updates will be on this web page. This policy was last updated in October 2026.

How to contact us
If you have any issues with our privacy policy, if you think your data has been used unfairly, or if you have any query regarding our data usage then please contact us via our site.
